Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about checking your gift card balance.

Where do I find my card number?

Your 16-digit card number is printed on the front of your gift card, typically in raised or flat-printed digits.

Where is the PIN located?

The 3 or 4-digit PIN is on the back of your card. You may need to scratch off a silver coating to reveal it. This PIN is required to check your balance.

Is it safe to check my balance here?

Yes. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption for all data transmissions. Your card details are never stored on our servers after the balance check is complete.

Why does my card show a zero balance?

A zero balance can occur if the card has not been activated, the funds have been fully spent, or the card has expired. Please verify the activation status with your original receipt.

What card types are supported?

We support Visa and Mastercard prepaid gift cards. American Express cards may require checking your balance through their own website.

Is there a fee to check my balance?

No. Checking your gift card balance on ThePerfectGift is completely free of charge. There are no hidden fees.
